Data deletion

Last updated: September 12, 2026

You can request deletion without signing in. Email Daniela Bauer at daniela@dnmpss.com with the subject “Shoutrrr data deletion request”.

Request deletion by email

  1. Include the email address used for Shoutrrr and the relevant social platform and account handle, if applicable.
  2. Say whether you want to remove a particular connected account and its data, specific content, or your full Shoutrrr account and associated data.
  3. If you cannot access your original sign-in email, explain that in the request. The operator may ask you to confirm control of the affected account before removing data.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, recovery codes, or identity documents in the initial request. This is an email request to the operator; submitting it does not itself delete data. Ask for a status update in the same email conversation if needed.

Requests cover information held by this Shoutrrr instance, including applicable profile information, social-account identifiers and credentials, imported platform data, and associated content. Where a request affects a shared workspace, the operator will confirm the scope and account authority before removing other members’ data.

Disconnect a social account yourself

  1. Sign in to Shoutrrr and open Accounts.
  2. Find the connected social account and choose Disconnect.
  3. To revoke the platform’s authorization as well, remove Shoutrrr in that platform’s connected-app or permissions settings. For Google and YouTube, use Google Account connections.

Disconnecting removes the connection and its stored credentials from the active Shoutrrr application. It is separate from deleting all workspace data or revoking the platform-side grant. Contact the operator if you also want related drafts, uploaded files, or imported data removed.

Delete your Shoutrrr account yourself

  1. Sign in and open Settings → Profile.
  2. Under Delete account, review the confirmation and enter your current Shoutrrr password.
  3. Confirm deletion only when you are ready to remove your profile and the associated account resources.

If you sign in only with Google and do not have a Shoutrrr password, use the email request above. The current self-service deletion form requires a Shoutrrr password.

If you are the only owner of a workspace with other members, account deletion is blocked until ownership is transferred or the workspace is deleted. Deleting your account can also remove workspaces you own that have no other members. Contact the operator if you need help resolving ownership or deciding what to retain.

What deletion does not undo

Deleting or disconnecting in Shoutrrr does not automatically remove posts, comments, videos, or other content already published on Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, or another platform. Remove that content on the relevant platform. Shoutrrr also cannot remove copies other people or services already hold.

Removing data from the active application may not immediately remove it from operational logs or any backup copies. Such copies are handled through the applicable normal retention or backup cycle. No specific completion or backup-removal time is guaranteed on this page.
