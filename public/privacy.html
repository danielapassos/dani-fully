Privacy policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy covers the private Shoutrrr creator workspace operated by Daniela Bauer. Public registration is closed.

Who operates this workspace

Daniela Bauer operates Shoutrrr at https://shoutrrr.app. For privacy questions or data requests, email daniela@dnmpss.com.

Information the workspace handles

  • Sign-in and membership: your name, email address, profile image, sign-in provider identifiers, workspace membership, and account preferences. If you set a Shoutrrr password, the app stores a password hash.
  • Connected social accounts: platform account identifiers, handles, profile details, permissions you grant, and access or refresh tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Content and activity: drafts, captions, uploaded images and videos, schedules, publishing results, and content you choose to share. When the relevant features are enabled and authorized, the app can also receive post and account metrics, comments, replies, and messages from connected platforms.
  • Technical information: session cookies, account-session records, and operational logs used to run the service and investigate errors. Hosting services may process request details such as IP addresses and browser information.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, manage workspace access, show connected accounts, prepare and schedule content, carry out publishing actions you authorize, display available analytics and conversations, and troubleshoot the service. Which platform information is available depends on the permissions you grant and the features enabled for this workspace.

Connecting a social account gives Shoutrrr the permissions shown in that platform’s authorization flow. It does not require you to give Shoutrrr your Google, Instagram, Threads, or TikTok password.

Where information is shared

The service is hosted on Laravel Cloud. Hosting and configured storage providers process the application’s data to operate it. Authorized workspace members can access information according to their workspace permissions, and the operator can access information needed to administer the service or handle a support request.

When you connect or publish to a platform, the information needed for that action is exchanged with that platform. Content you publish is subject to the audience and privacy settings of the destination platform. Anyone with a public share link you create may be able to view the material included in that link.

Profile images and optional external media tools can make requests to their respective providers. If an optional integration is enabled and you use it, the information needed for that feature is sent to that provider.

Connected platforms handle information under their own policies. Shoutrrr uses YouTube API Services for its YouTube integration. For Google and YouTube, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or revoke Google access on the Google security settings page, in addition to requesting deletion from Shoutrrr.

Storage and deletion

Workspace data is retained to support the workspace and its authorized connections. Disconnecting a social account removes that connection and its stored credentials from the active application; it does not by itself delete all drafts, uploaded files, or other workspace data.

To request removal of your account information, connected-platform data, or associated content, follow the data-deletion instructions. Removal from the active application does not necessarily remove every copy immediately. Where backups or operational logs contain information, those copies are handled through the applicable normal retention or backup cycle; no fixed removal time is promised here.

Disconnecting or deleting your Shoutrrr account does not automatically remove content already published on a social platform. Manage that content on the platform itself.

Your choices and questions

You can review your profile, disconnect a social account, revoke permissions in the platform’s settings, or request access, correction, or deletion by emailing daniela@dnmpss.com. We may need to confirm your control of the relevant account before acting. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or identity documents in an initial request.

Changes to this policy

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Contact the operator if you have questions about how a change applies to your information.
